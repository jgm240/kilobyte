What it is

Kilobyte is a small Linux system, made from Debian, whose desktop is drawn entirely with coloured characters: box-drawing lines, block characters and shadows, like the text-mode programs of the 1980s. It has no graphics server and no toolkit. It is shell scripts on top of dialog and classic text-mode programs, plus a few small Python programs for games, Paint, the screen saver and the movable windows of the Kilobyte Desktop.

It starts in seconds, runs in 50 MB of memory, and is at home on old PCs, a Raspberry Pi 1, a laptop that only starts from CDs, and an e-ink Kindle.

Today (weather, appointments, headlines), Dictionary and translator, Encyclopedia (Wikipedia without the Internet), Sync between your computers
Media
Video player (as coloured blocks, or the real picture), Music player, Pictures, DVDs and CDs, Paint
Games
Chess, Sudoku, text adventures, Mines, Solitaire, Reversi, Moon Buggy, Snake, Robots, Hangman, Adventure
Old programs
DOS programs and games (DOSBox), Windows programs (Wine), and a Store of text-mode programs from Debian
