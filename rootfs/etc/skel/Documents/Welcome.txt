Welcome to Kilobyte!
====================

This is the Editor (mcedit). It works much like EDIT.COM:

  F9            open the menu bar at the top
  F2            save
  F10 or Esc    close the editor
  Shift+arrows  select text
  F5 / F6 / F8  copy / move / delete the selected block
  F7            search

Your files live in ~/Documents. Try the Spreadsheet next: open Budget.sc.
