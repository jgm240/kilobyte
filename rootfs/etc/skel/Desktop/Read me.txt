This is your desktop.

Every file and folder in the folder "Desktop" of your home folder shows up
as an icon on the desktop of Kilobyte Windows. Double-click an icon to open
it; click it with the right mouse button for more (open with another
program, move it to the trash).

To put something here: in the file manager press F2 and choose "Put on the
desktop", or save or copy it into ~/Desktop.

The icons of programs are chosen in Settings > Desktop icons. You may
delete this file.
